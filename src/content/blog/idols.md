<p>Who you admire impacts your life more than it might seem.</p>
<p>Your idols define what is possible within the human condition.</p>
<p>They set a bar for you. Their achievements reach out to you and say
    ”<em>if I
        did this, you can too</em>”.</p>
<p>That’s why we resonate most with idols who grew up in similar circumstances. It silences the doubts about
    your starting point.</p>
<p>Take someone overweight, unemployed, who grew up in a low-income household.</p>
<p>They can’t relate to a successful athlete who went to an Ivy League school, because they can hand-wave
    away their success to
    “<em>starting early</em>”,
    and “<em>having rich parents</em>”.</p>
<p>But when they hear about someone like David Goggins, who also struggled with their weight and came from a
    low-income
    household, they can hold this up as an example of what’s possible—“<em>If he did it from where he was,
        I
        can do it from
        where I’m from</em>”.</p>
<p>That’s the bright side of idol selection.</p>
<p>The dark side is those who don’t actually want to find anyone in a similar situation who has done
    anything great.</p>
<p>They’re not looking for a comparable situation to gauge what’s possible—they’re looking for an
    excuse to
    justify
    their <a target="_blank" href="/blog/normal" rel="noopener">mediocrity</a>.</p>
<p><mark>The light of success from these figures shines a spotlight on their failures.</mark>
</p>
<p>It tells them “<em>You’re not everything you could be, and you know it</em>”.</p>
<p>As the spotlight’s intensity increases, the individual has a choice: pull themselves up, or bring others
    down.
</p>
<p>And in <a target="_blank" href="/blog/reject-modernity-embrace-masculinity" rel="noopener">modernity</a>, many have
    chosen the latter.
</p>
<p>The media’s emphasis on the failures of successful people fosters a culture of negativity and
    excuse-making instead
    of aspiration and growth.</p>
<p>The seminal achievements of history’s greatest entrepreneurs and inventors are only used as
    pointers
    to
    identify them
    before we are bombarded with their apparent litany of shortcomings.</p>
<p>
</p><blockquote>“You know those founding father guys owned slaves?”</blockquote>
<p></p>
<p>
</p><blockquote>“You know the guy who invented Ford was a Nazi sympathizer?”</blockquote>
<p></p>
<p>
</p><blockquote>“You know the founder of Apple was mean to his employees?”</blockquote>
<p></p>
<p>Contemporary entrepreneurs are not spared from this assault.</p>
<p>I made several stones bleed before finding favorable articles on Elon Musk or Jeff Bezos.
</p>
<p>If you think this hatred is warranted, or that being called a ‘<em>billionaire</em>’ is a slur, then you
    have
    been
    successfully psyoped.</p>
<p>I can only hazard to guess the unspeakable horrors that lie within the closets of the people who make
    these claims.
</p>
<p><strong>Do not let these weasels kill your idols.</strong></p>
<p>When you hear them speak of the faults of others, recognize it for what it is: an attempt to bring down
    those who
    have succeeded in order to justify their own lack of achievement.</p>
<p>These detractors are not interested in the truth or balanced perspectives. They are driven by envy and
    insecurity, and they <a target="_blank" href="/blog/change" rel="noopener">don’t want to change</a>.
</p>
<p>Their aim is to undermine the credibility and accomplishments of successful individuals, not to provide
    constructive
    criticism or meaningful insight.</p>
<p>Instead of letting these negative narratives shape your view, seek out the positive lessons and
    inspiration that
    successful people can offer.</p>
<p>Idolizing someone does not mean ignoring their faults, it means appreciating their journey
    and the
    lessons they provide.</p>
<p>Stay critical of the sources of negativity and always question the motives behind such attacks.</p>
<p>Find role models who resonate with you and look up to them for the right reasons—their perseverance,
    their
    innovations, and their ability to overcome obstacles.</p>
<p>You can draw strength and motivation from their stories, using them as a beacon to guide your own path to
    success.
</p>
<p>It is your perspective and attitude that determine how much you can learn and grow from those you admire.
</p>
<p>Choose to be inspired, not disheartened, and let your idols lift you to new heights.</p>
<p><strong>Do not let them kill your ambition.</strong></p>
