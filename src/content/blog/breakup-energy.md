<p>Humans have discovered four fundamental forces in the universe:</p>
<ol id="ul-nm1">
    <li>Gravity</li>
    <li>Electromagnetism</li>
    <li>Strong nuclear force</li>
    <li>Weak nuclear force</li>
</ol>
<p>Later on, we realized that these four forces alone couldn’t explain everything.</p>
<p>We discovered two missing components:
</p>
<ol start="5" id="ul-nm2">
    <li>Matter particles (<em>quarks and leptons</em>)</li>
    <li>Higgs boson</li>
</ol>
<picture class="intrinsic intrinsic--55">
    <img class="intrinsic-item" src="/img/theory-of-everything-formula-incomplete.png" alt="The theory of everything formula is incomplete." width="1656" height="911" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
</picture>
<p>But even these forces combined could not explain certain phenomena.</p>
<p>We observe that under certain conditions, humans can break free from the
    constraints of these forces and create a new type of energy.</p>
<p>This energy is so powerful that it puts into question whether the other forces even exist.</p>
<p>It is difficult to study this force in a lab, because it is impossible to artificially create.</p>
<p>We know it exists though. Scientists have documented its distinctive signatures.</p>
<p>3 a.m. runs through empty streets.</p>
<p>Punching bags reduced to tatters.</p>
<p>Holes in the dry wall.</p>
<p>Feats of strength thought impossible days prior.</p>
<p>Levels of productivity unexplainable by the known forces.</p>
<p>Some of the initial conditions include bruised ego, unexpected abandonment, and betrayal.
    Demon-like levels of
    malice act as a catalyst.</p>
<p>We hereby propose the term “<em>breakup energy</em>” to describe this phenomenon, adding it to our
    collective
    theory of
    everything.</p>
<picture class="intrinsic intrinsic--55">
    <img class="intrinsic-item" src="/img/breakup-energy-formula.png" alt="The theory of everything formula is incomplete." width="1656" height="911" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
</picture>
<p>If you have experienced this force yourself, you have no doubt regarding its existence.</p>
<p>If you are lucky enough to be experiencing this force right now, <strong>do not waste it</strong>.</p>
<p>You’ll feel like it will always be there. But it won’t.</p>
<p>It will eventually pass through you. And you won’t be able to bring it back.</p>
<p>This energy behaves like radioactive decay—we can’t predict exactly when it will disappear, but we know
    it
    has a half-life.</p>
<p>Some people get days. Some get weeks. The lucky bastards get months.</p>
<p>But this decay doesn’t negotiate. It follows its own timeline, indifferent to your schedule or readiness.
</p>
<p>Every hour you spend bitching, replaying, and relitigating the past is converting the energy to
    resentment
    instead of results (pathetic).</p>
<p>So don’t waste it. Embrace it.</p>
<p>Go for a run and transform the energy into cardiovascular fitness.</p>
<p>Go to the gym and transform the energy into muscle mass.</p>
<p>Go to work and transform the energy into your bottom line.</p>
<p>Then when the energy is gone, a hazy memory at best, you’ll still have remnants of its existence.</p>
<p>The body you built, the skills you sharpened, the income you earned—permanent trophies from an ephemeral
    reaction.</p>
<p>The clock is ticking.</p>
